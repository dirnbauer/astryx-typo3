A payment mark is a promise about what happens at the till, so the name of the method is always text: the artwork carries it as its alt, and a method with no artwork uploaded yet is set as type rather than left as a gap. The marks share one height, because a wide wordmark and a square emblem set to the same width read as two different sizes. Conditions that need words — delivery times, surcharges, minimum order — belong in the note, not on a mark nobody can read.
