A payment mark is a promise about what happens at the till, so the name of the method is always text: the artwork carries the name as its alt, and a method with no artwork uploaded yet is set as a Token rather than left as a gap. The artwork is a Logo in its original colours — a payment network's mark is not to be greyed — and the Logo gives every mark one height, because a wide wordmark and a square emblem set to the same width read as two different sizes. The marks are a horizontal List; the band is always a tight one, and compact draws the marks closer together. Conditions that need words — delivery times, surcharges, minimum order — belong in the note, not on a mark nobody can read. {data -> f:render.text(field: 'note')} {mark.title}
