One pair per credit: what is being credited is the term, who holds the right to it is its description. A licence short name is a second description on the same term rather than a line of its own, because "CC BY 4.0" is a property of that credit and not a separate fact. The licence chip is text in a Badge, so a reader who cannot see the tint still gets the licence read out with the credit it belongs to. The list is the small MetadataList with a rule under each credit; the stylesheet flows it down the columns rather than across them, so a long list of credits reads down each column. `columns` is passed on so the list knows it is laid out in several, and keeps the rule under every pair.
{credit -> f:render.text(field: 'title')}
{credit -> f:render.text(field: 'text')}
{credit -> f:render.text(field: 'value')}
