Tabs, TabList, Tab and TabPanel, wired by astryx.js (data-g-tabs): arrow keys move between tabs, Home and End jump, and only the selected tab is a tab stop. Every panel is in the markup and none starts hidden, so the band is indexed, printed and searched in full; the script is what takes the other panels out — without it the page is a stack of sections, never broken. The strip scrolls sideways on a phone rather than wrapping, which is TabList's own doing. Each panel keeps its title on screen (`titleDisplay="visible"`): here the panel is the capability and its heading belongs to it. The title is passed as the plain field, because TabPanel escapes its arguments. {data -> f:render.text(field: 'eyebrow')} {data -> f:render.text(field: 'header')} {data -> f:render.text(field: 'lead')} {panel.title} {panel -> f:render.text(field: 'text')} {panel.title}
