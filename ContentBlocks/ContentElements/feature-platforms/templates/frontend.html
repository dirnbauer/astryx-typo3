A Grid list of Items: the platform's small tile at the start, its name and the version or requirement underneath. The requirement sits under the name rather than beside it, because a requirement read as a subtitle is read and one read as a suffix is not. A platform with a link is an interactive Item, which is itself the anchor, so the whole row is the target; Item renders that anchor in place of its li, which is why the list item is written around it here. One without a link is the Item's own li.
