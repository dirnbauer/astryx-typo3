An ordered List with numbered tiles, so the sequence survives without styles and the number on each step is its position: reordering the rows in the backend renumbers them, and no editor is ever left maintaining a sequence by hand. The List lays the steps out in two to four columns and reflows to one on a phone. The icon is optional and sits above the heading; the number is always shown. A step that continues on a page of its own links its title there, rather than repeating the title as a second link underneath. Everything here is composed of components, so the element ships no stylesheet of its own. {data -> f:render.text(field: 'eyebrow')} {data -> f:render.text(field: 'header')} {data -> f:render.text(field: 'lead')} {item.title} {item -> f:render.text(field: 'title')} {item -> f:render.text(field: 'text')}
