Every row is an Item, and an Item given a link field IS the link: the whole row is the target, so somebody aiming at four words on a phone does not have to hit them exactly, and the hover tint, the pressed state and the focus ring are the row's own. The chevron sits in the Item's end region and is hidden from assistive technology — it says "this goes somewhere", which the link already said.
