Figures are stored as text, not as numbers, so an editor can write 12k or 12,400 and keep whichever register the page needs. Formatting a stored integer would take that decision away and get the thousands separator wrong in at least one of the languages this catalog ships. A MetadataList of Stats: each label is the term and moves with its figure as one cell, and the Stat shows the figure above its label without reversing the markup.
