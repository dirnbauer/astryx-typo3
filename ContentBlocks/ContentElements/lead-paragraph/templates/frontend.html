The lead is a plain paragraph in the large type step, not a heading at a smaller weight: it is the first sentence of the piece, and a screen reader that hears it announced as a heading loses the boundary between the title and the text. SectionIntro sets it in the primary colour here, because it is the text itself rather than a line introducing other text, and keeps it to a reading measure. A standfirst is short enough that centring stays legible — which is why this element centres its text when asked and Rich Text does not. The band is a tight one: it exists to introduce whatever comes next, so it gives back the air under it. Everything is composed of components, so the element ships no stylesheet of its own. {data -> f:render.text(field: 'header')} {data -> f:render.text(field: 'lead')}
