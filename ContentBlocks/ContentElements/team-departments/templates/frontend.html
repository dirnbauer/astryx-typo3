One element is one department. Stacking several of them gives the organisation its shape and lets each band carry its own tone, which a single element holding every department could not do. The Divider under the department's SectionIntro sits with the intro rather than in a band of its own, so it can never outlive the heading it belongs to. Each person is a level-three heading under the department's level two, set at the size of a card title. Compact tightens the band and the grid's gap, and leaves out the line under each role — in the markup, not just on screen — for a band that sits between two others. The alt names the person: these are cards in a band whose heading is the department, and a face without a name would be the second unlabelled thing on the row.
