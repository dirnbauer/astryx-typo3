One element is one department. Stacking several of them gives the organisation its shape and lets each band carry its own tone, which a single element holding every department could not do. The rule under the department title is a border on the intro rather than a separate divider element, so it can never outlive the heading it belongs to. The alt names the person: these are cards in a band whose heading is the department, and a face without a name would be the second unlabelled thing on the row.
