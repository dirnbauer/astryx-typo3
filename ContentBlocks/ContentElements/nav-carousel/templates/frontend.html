The row is the Carousel component: a scroll-snap track, so dragging, momentum, keyboard scrolling and the snap points are the browser's, and the scrollbar stays visible because it is the first thing that tells a mouse user there is more to the side. The two arrows exist because they are given names — Carousel renders a control only once it has a label — and the shared runtime pages the track by one card per press, switches an arrow off at its end of the row, and shows the pair only to a fine pointer on a row that does not fit. The status label is what a screen reader hears once a scroll comes to rest: "Card 3 of 8". Without the script the arrows stay hidden and the track still scrolls.
