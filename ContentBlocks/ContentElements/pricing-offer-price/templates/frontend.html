A line through a figure is a visual convention and nothing more: read aloud it disappears, and the two amounts become one confusing pair. Each therefore carries a named label, and the strike is left to do the seeing. The deadline is written out rather than counted down. A clock would have to be scripted, would be wrong in a cached page, and a date a reader can note in a diary is more use than seconds ticking away.
