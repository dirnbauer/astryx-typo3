The band is its own sticky element — no scroll listener, no fixed overlay and nothing that outlives the column it was placed in. Because it stays in the flow it can never be reached by Tab out of order, and it releases at the foot of the content instead of sitting on top of the page footer. While it floats it covers the bottom edge of whatever scrolls past, so it only floats where it is one slim row, and the page's scroll padding keeps a focused control clear of it. The heading is an h2 like every band's, set at a small size: the bar names an offer, it does not open a chapter.
