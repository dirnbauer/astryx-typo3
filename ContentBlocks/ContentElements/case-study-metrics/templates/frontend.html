A description list, because that is what this band is: a set of measures and the values they took. The list is a MetadataList with the editor's column count, and every measure is a Stat inside it — the label as the term, the figure with its unit as the description, the line on how it was measured as a note. The Stat shows the figure above its label without reversing the source, and rules each figure off at the top. The note under the band carries the measurement period.
