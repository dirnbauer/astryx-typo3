A store badge is supplied artwork with mandatory wording and clear space, so it is uploaded as an image and never redrawn as a styled button. Where no artwork has been uploaded the entry falls back to a plain link rather than to something shaped like a badge but not one. The badge is one link with one name: the store's title, which is also what the artwork says. An uploaded file's own alternative text describes the file, and a badge whose alt reads like a photo caption is a link nobody can find by the store's name.
