Only the portrait and the name are rendered. Roles, biographies and the child's own text are ignored on purpose: the point of a wall is the number of faces, and two hundred people each carrying a job title is a directory, not an acknowledgement. The portraits carry an empty alt. Every name is printed directly under its own picture, so a meaningful alt would read two hundred names twice — the one place in this group where naming the face costs more than it gives.
