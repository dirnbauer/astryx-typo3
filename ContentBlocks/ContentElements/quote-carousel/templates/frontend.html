The track is Carousel's scroll-snap row, so it already works with no script at all — drag, momentum and keyboard scrolling are the browser's. The runtime adds the two arrows a mouse user has no other way to reach (shown only where there is something to page to), and says "Quote 2 of 3" to a screen reader once a scroll comes to rest. Each card holds a Figure: the words in a plain Blockquote, which needs no accent rule on a card and draws the quotation marks of the page's language, and the person in a Citation with their portrait. The portrait's alt stays empty because the name is printed beside it.
