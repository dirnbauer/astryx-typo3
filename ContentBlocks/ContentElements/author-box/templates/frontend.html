The band is sized to sit inside an article column: it never sets its own width wider than the prose it follows, which is why the byline is capped here rather than left to the width field alone. The name is the block's own heading at level three, set at the size of a card title. An author box is a subsection of the article above it, not a band of the page, so it must not reach for level two and break the article's outline. Boxed puts a Divider between the article and the byline; compact drops it, asks the avatar for its medium size and sets the two lines about the author as supporting text, so the byline sits straight under the last paragraph.
