The whole row is the link, not just the job title: a vacancy is one target, and somebody reading on a phone should not have to hit four words exactly. The row's description carries the location and the contract type, because that is the pair of facts an applicant filters on before reading anything else — and it is the one place an editor can keep them true. A role with no link is still rendered, as plain text: a vacancy that has been announced but whose posting is not written yet is a real state, and a dead link would be worse than no link.
