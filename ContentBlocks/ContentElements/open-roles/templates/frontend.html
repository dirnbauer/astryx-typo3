The whole row is the link, not just the job title: a vacancy is one target, and somebody reading on a phone should not have to hit four words exactly. Item resolves the link field itself and renders the row as an — inside a list item of its own, since a link cannot be the
  • . A chevron at the end says the row goes somewhere. The job title is the row's label at the large size and a heavier weight, not a heading in the page outline. The row's description carries the location and the contract type, because that is the pair of facts an applicant filters on before reading anything else — and it is the one place an editor can keep them true. A role with no link is still rendered, as plain text: a vacancy that has been announced but whose posting is not written yet is a real state, and a dead link would be worse than no link.
