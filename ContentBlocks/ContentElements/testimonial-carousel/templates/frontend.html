The track is a scroll-snap row, so it is already a carousel with no script at all — swipe, drag, momentum and the arrow keys belong to the browser. Molecule/Carousel renders the track, the two arrows and the status line that tells a screen reader which quote it has come to; astryx.js pages the rail, switches each arrow off at its end and hides both where the rail fits or a finger is the pointer. From tablet width two whole cards sit side by side and the arrows say that the row continues; a card cut off there would read as a layout fault. On a phone there are no arrows, so the next card shows a sliver at the edge instead — the one sign left that there is more to swipe to. Each card is a figure — the words in a plain Blockquote with the marks of the page's language, the person in a Citation.
