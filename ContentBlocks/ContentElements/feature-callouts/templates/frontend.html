The markers belong to the artwork, not to the template: drawing them over a responsive image means guessing at coordinates that were true for one width. The legend is a List with numbered tiles (marker="step", an ordered list): the list counts the entries itself, so its numbers follow the editor's order, and the ordered list is what tells a screen reader that order.
