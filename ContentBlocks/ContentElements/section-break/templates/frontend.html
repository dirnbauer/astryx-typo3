The rule is Divider, an hr: a thematic break, which is exactly what this element is. The words under it are a SectionIntro with a real h2, so the break is in the outline as well as on screen and a reader who jumps between headings lands on the new part. The line under the heading is the intro's lead at the supporting step. The gap under the rule is larger than the gap between the words, so the line reads as the top edge of what follows rather than as a separator floating between two parts. Centred, the rule shrinks to a short mark over the title: a full-width line over a centred heading reads as a page divider, not as part of the title. {data -> f:render.text(field: 'eyebrow')} {data -> f:render.text(field: 'header')} {data -> f:render.text(field: 'subheader')}
