The current step is the child whose link resolves to this page, or the one whose link the editor left empty. Everything before it is complete, everything after it is still to come. The steps are a Stepper of Steps, the component made for exactly this: an ordered list, because the order is the whole message, with upstream's three states — complete, current, upcoming. The current step carries aria-current="step"; the other two spell their state out in a hidden status line, because a filled circle and a hollow one are not information anybody can hear. The circle holds the step's number. Completed and upcoming steps keep their links, so a reader can go back to a finished stage or look ahead; the current step is plain text.
