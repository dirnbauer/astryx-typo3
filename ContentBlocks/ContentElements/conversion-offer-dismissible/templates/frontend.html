The only behaviour in the group, and it is attributes: the runtime in astryx.js removes whatever carries data-g-dismissible when a data-g-dismiss control inside it is pressed, moves the keyboard on to the next control, and remembers the decision for thirty days under the band's dismiss key. All three hooks are the components' — Section's `dismissible` and `dismissKey` write the first two and Banner's the third — so they can never drift apart in a template that copies only one of them. The bar sits in the flow of the page. The corner card floats over it, at the foot of the window, until it is closed; the key is the element's uid, so closing one offer never hides another.
