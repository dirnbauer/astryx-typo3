The rows are a List in the editor's number of columns, with a rule under every row, so a column reads as one list rather than as a stack of pairs. Compact asks the List for its balanced density and the avatar for its small size. The years stay plain text rather than becoming a element. "2011–2019" is not a machine-readable datetime, and dressing it up as one would put a promise into the markup that nothing can keep; Timestamp without a datetime renders a span, which is honest.
