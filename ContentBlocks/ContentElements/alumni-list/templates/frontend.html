A row reads as a sentence from left to right: the face, the name, the years they were here, then where they went. The child's role carries the years and its text carries the current position, and the source order is that order — which is the only arrangement that survives being read aloud. The years stay plain text rather than becoming a element. "2011–2019" is not a machine-readable datetime, and dressing it up as one would put a promise into the markup that nothing can keep. The alt names the person: an alumni list is a page somebody searches for a former colleague on, and a face that answers to nothing helps nobody.
