The prose column caps at 65 characters a line even in the wide variant. A founder story that runs the full page width does not get read, and the width field here decides how much room the picture gets rather than how long the lines are. The name closes the story rather than opening it: the reader is meant to arrive at the person, not to be introduced to them before there is any reason to care. A Divider sets the signature off from the last paragraph. The portrait is a Figure with its caption, cropped to one portrait ratio by AspectRatio, so a founder who sent a landscape snapshot still fits the column. The caption is written last into the figure's own slot, and only when there is one, so a blank caption field leaves no empty figcaption behind. The alt names the founder — this is a portrait, and it is the only picture on the band.
