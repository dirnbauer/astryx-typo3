The portrait, the Blockquote and the Citation are all direct children of the Figure: a figcaption may only be the figure's first or last child, so the credit cannot sit in a wrapper beside the portrait. The stylesheet places the three on a grid instead. The portrait carries an empty alt: the name is printed directly beside it, and a screen reader that also announces "photograph of …" reads the person out twice for no added information. Blockquote sets the words at its featured step and draws the quotation marks of the page's language; the credit lines up with the words, not with the accent rule. Centred, the rule goes and the credit follows.
