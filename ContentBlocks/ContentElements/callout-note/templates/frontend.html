A Banner, which is an aside: the passage is related to the text around it but is not part of its argument, so a reader who skips it loses nothing of the thread. The icon the editor picks also sets the Banner's tone, so the one choice says whether the note informs, reassures, warns or forbids: an info glyph tints it as information, a tick as success, a triangle as a warning and a cross as an error. Any other glyph keeps the neutral tone. The tint carries the meaning on its own; the glyph is decoration and is hidden from screen readers, as Banner's icon slot expects. `tone` is the band the note sits on, like every other element's.
