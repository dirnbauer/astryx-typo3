One collection, sorted into two columns by the child's own toggle, so an editor moves an argument from one side to the other by flipping a switch rather than by cutting and pasting between two lists. The two markers are a tick and a cross, not two colours: on a monochrome printout and to anyone who cannot tell two hues apart, the glyph is what still says which side a row is on. Icon tints them in the theme's status colours on top. The glyphs are decoration to a screen reader, which hears the column heading instead.
