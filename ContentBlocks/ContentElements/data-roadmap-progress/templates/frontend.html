An ordered sequence of stages with a state each is what Stepper and Step are: the list is an ol, the current stage carries aria-current="step", and the state is said in words to assistive technology as well as drawn by the indicator. The editor's icon key is the state: check-circle is a finished stage, clock one under way, anything else a planned one. Each stage is a section of the page, so its name is a real h3, with the percentage beside it as Text and the shared ProgressBar under both as the drawing. The percentage says in figures what the indicator says in colour, and Stepper joins the indicators with its own rule.
