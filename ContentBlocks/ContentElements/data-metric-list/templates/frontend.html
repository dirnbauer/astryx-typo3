No bars and no icons on purpose. This is the element that has to survive a 300px sidebar, and every decoration that would fit at full width is the first thing to break there. The heading is the band's own, so it is an h2 at the size of a small heading rather than an h3 under nothing. Labels, figures and details are Text; the rules between the rows are this element's own, because the figure set against the far edge only reads as belonging to its label with a line under both. Where the band is wide enough the rows flow into two columns rather than leaving most of it empty. {data -> f:render.text(field: 'header')} {data -> f:render.text(field: 'lead')}
