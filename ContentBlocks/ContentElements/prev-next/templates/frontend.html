Previous comes first in the source and on the page, because that is the order the two directions are read in. Either half may be empty — the ends of a series are exactly where this element earns its keep — and the half that is left then spans the band on its own. Each half is a clickable Card with one link, and the page title is its accessible name; the word "Previous" or "Next" is an Eyebrow above it rather than part of the link, so nobody hears "next" twice in a row. The chevron beside it is an Icon in the eyebrow's accent colour and points the way the link goes. The next half is aligned to the far edge (Card align="end"), so the pair reads outwards from the middle.
