A Grid list of Cards, one measured result each, and in each card a Stat: the figure in the accent colour with its unit on the same baseline, what it counts as the term, and the line that names the feature behind it as the note. Stat is a description list, term first in the source, so a figure read out of context still says what it counts; on screen the figure comes first. That line under each figure is what separates this band from a statistics strip: a number nobody can attribute to anything is a number nobody believes. The child's icon is deliberately not drawn: the figure is the picture.
