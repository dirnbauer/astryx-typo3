Nothing here reads the clock. No day is marked as today and no "open now" badge is drawn, because this page is cached: a badge rendered at three in the afternoon would still claim to be open at midnight, and a schedule that lies once is a schedule nobody trusts again. Exceptions belong in the note, where a human keeps them true. A closed day says the word as well as striking the hours through, so the state survives being read aloud. The schedule is the small MetadataList with a rule under each day; the stylesheet only sets day and hours side by side, with the line of text under them.
{row -> f:render.text(field: 'title')}
{row -> f:render.text(field: 'value')} {row.value}
{row -> f:render.text(field: 'text')} {data -> f:render.text(field: 'note')}
