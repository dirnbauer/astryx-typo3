A mark is a name a reader recognises, so its alt is the organisation and never "logo": a screen reader announcing "image, logo" ten times in a row has told nobody which customers these are. Every mark is an Atom/Logo: one height for all of them, grey and faint at rest so no single brand pulls the wall towards itself, and full strength when a reader points at a linked one or tabs to it. Without artwork the Logo sets the name as type instead — a wall with three gaps in it is worse proof than a wall of words.
