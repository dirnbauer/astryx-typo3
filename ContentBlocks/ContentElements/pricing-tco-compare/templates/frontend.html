Cost of ownership as figures. One description list (Grid as dl) holding one Stat per figure: the label is the term, the figure with its unit and the basis it was measured on are its descriptions, so a label never drifts away from its number, and the unit is read out with the figure. Every figure needs its source or a stated assumption in that third line; an unsourced cost claim is the fastest way to lose a tender.
