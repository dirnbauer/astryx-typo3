The heading is the session title, not the speaker's name: a programme page made of these should read as a list of talks. The name lives in the author field and is announced under the abstract, where a reader has already been given a reason to care who is saying it. The badge is the slot or the stage — a marker, not a link. It is set before the heading in the source because "Keynote, 09:30" is what a reader skimming a programme is looking for. The alt names the speaker: this is the one portrait on the band and it stands away from the name rather than beside it.
