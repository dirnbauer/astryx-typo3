The heading is the session title, not the speaker's name: a programme page made of these should read as a list of talks. The name lives in the author field and is announced under the abstract, where a reader has already been given a reason to care who is saying it. The badge is the slot or the stage — a marker, not a link. It is set before the heading in the source because "Keynote, 09:30" is what a reader skimming a programme is looking for. On an accent band an accent badge would vanish into its own background, so the template asks Badge for its outline variant there instead of repainting it. The portrait is cropped square by AspectRatio.
