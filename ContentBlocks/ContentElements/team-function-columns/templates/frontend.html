The columns are not a field: they come out of the child records themselves, grouped on the role and ordered by first appearance. An editor therefore controls both the headings and their order by sorting the people, and adding a function means typing it on a person rather than editing this element. A function is a level-three heading that wears the Eyebrow: the outline gets a real heading per column, and the column reads as a labelled list rather than as a run of competing titles. A Divider runs under it. No portraits and no per-person text at all. Credits panels and discipline lists are read by scanning names, and a face or a sentence beside every one of eighty names turns a list into a wall. Compact asks the List for its tightest rows and its small text size. A person with no function typed on them still belongs on the page, so the group renders — it just has no heading rather than an empty one. {function} {person.title} {person -> f:render.text(field: 'title')}
