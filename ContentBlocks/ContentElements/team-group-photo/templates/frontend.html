The alt comes from the file rather than from a field on this element. A group portrait is not one person, so there is nothing here that could name it — the editor who uploaded the picture is the only one who knows who is in the frame and where they are standing. The name key is set small but it is never hidden, on any width. It is the only place the people in the photograph are named, and dropping it on a phone would take that away from exactly the readers most likely to be looking for themselves in it. Full bleed is not a field on this element: it is the 'Full bleed' width, which every band in the catalog already answers to.
