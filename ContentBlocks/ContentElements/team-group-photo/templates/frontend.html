The alt comes from the file rather than from a field on this element. A group portrait is not one person, so there is nothing here that could name it — the editor who uploaded the picture is the only one who knows who is in the frame and where they are standing. The photograph keeps its own proportions: a group portrait is composed around where the people are standing, and cropping it to a fixed ratio is the one decision this element must not make for the photographer. So it is a Figure, not an AspectRatio; the Figure gives it its width and radius and sets the caption in the supporting style. The name key is set small but it is never hidden, on any width. It is the only place the people in the photograph are named, and dropping it on a phone would take that away from exactly the readers most likely to be looking for themselves in it. Full bleed is not a field on this element: it is the 'Full bleed' width, which every band in the catalog already answers to. {data -> f:render.text(field: 'caption')} {data -> f:render.text(field: 'note')} {data.cta_label}
