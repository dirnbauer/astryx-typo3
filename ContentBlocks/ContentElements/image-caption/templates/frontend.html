Caption and credit are one figcaption, not two: the credit belongs to the same picture, and a second block outside the figure would leave it orphaned for anyone reading the figure on its own. The caption reads at body size, the credit under it in supporting type. The picture keeps its own proportions. A chart or a screenshot cropped to a shape the element preferred would lose exactly the part the caption is about. Figure gives it the full width and the container's corners. The heading heads the band, so it sits above the figure as an h2 rather than inside it; SectionIntro centres it with the figure when asked. {data -> f:render.text(field: 'header')} {data -> f:render.text(field: 'caption')} {data -> f:render.text(field: 'note')}
