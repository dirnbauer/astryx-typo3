A plain anchor and nothing else: no scroll listener, no button that fades in after eight hundred pixels. #main-content is the same target the skip link at the top of every page uses, and
carries tabindex="-1", so following the link moves keyboard focus to the start of the content as well as the viewport. The document scrolls smoothly unless the visitor asked for reduced motion, and stops below the sticky header — both are the base stylesheet's (scroll-behavior and scroll-padding on html).
