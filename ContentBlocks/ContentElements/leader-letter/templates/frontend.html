The image slot takes either a signature scan or a small portrait, and whichever it is, it renders at the foot of the text and never at the head. A letter that opens with the sender's face is a profile; a letter is something you read and then find out who wrote it. That also settles the alt: the picture is announced with the name, because for a signature the name is the only sensible description and for a portrait it is the right one anyway.
