The cluster is decoration and is marked as such. Twenty overlapping portraits read to a screen reader as twenty graphics in front of the one sentence the band exists to deliver — and the fact they carry is the count, which the value and unit beside them already write out in words. This is the same rule as a progress bar drawn over a number: the picture is hidden, the number is not. The size is the group's: every avatar in an AvatarGroup takes it, so the discs and their overlap stay in step. Compact asks for the medium size. Only the portraits are rendered. Names, roles and links on the child records are ignored here; 'Contributor Wall' is the element for a cluster where every name should be legible. Centring is the section's choice.
