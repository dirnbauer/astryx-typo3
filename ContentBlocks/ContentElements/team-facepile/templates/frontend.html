The cluster is decoration and is marked as such. Twenty overlapping portraits read to a screen reader as twenty graphics in front of the one sentence the band exists to deliver — and the fact they carry is the count, which the value and unit beside them already write out in words. This is the same rule as a progress bar drawn over a number: the picture is hidden, the number is not. Only the portraits are rendered. Names, roles and links on the child records are ignored here; 'Contributor Wall' is the element for a cluster where every name should be legible.
