A nav landmark named by its own heading, so it can be reached and skipped as one thing. The landmark is the element's, not the Card's: Card is a box and renders a div, and a div is no landmark however it is labelled. The entries are curated rather than scraped from the headings on the page: an editor decides what belongs in the contents, and renaming a section never silently drops a line from it. The numbers are List's decimal markers on an ordered list, so they follow the order of the rows. An entry without a link is printed as text rather than as a link that goes nowhere.
