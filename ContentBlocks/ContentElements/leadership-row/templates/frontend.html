Round portraits at the shared avatar's largest size, rather than the framed rectangles of 'Team Grid': the two elements sit on the same page often enough that they have to be told apart at a glance. The alt names the person. The picture is the largest thing in the cell, so when it fails to load an empty alt would leave a silent hole where a face was meant to be — the two words cost less than that. The name carries the link. A second "get in touch" underneath would be a duplicate target with vaguer wording, and a heading that is already the person's name is the clearest accessible name a link can have.
