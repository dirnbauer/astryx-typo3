The rules between the links are drawn by the List, never typed into a label: a separator that lives in the text ends up in a copied line, in the page title of a bookmark and in what a screen reader announces between two perfectly ordinary link names. Wrapped onto several lines on a phone, the List drops them, because a rule at the start of a line marks no boundary. With the heading left empty the bar reads as one line, so the nav takes its accessible name from a translated default instead. The bar is always a tight band; compact tightens the list.
