Funding programmes prescribe both the wording and the clear space around their emblem, so the sentence stays an editable text rather than something this template composes, and the supplied artwork is never cropped, tinted or turned to greyscale — only scaled to a common height. The funder's name is the artwork's alt, because a funding notice that a screen reader renders as "image" has acknowledged nobody. Each emblem is a Logo in its original colours (on a light plate in the dark scheme); a funder without artwork is set as its name in the same Logo instead of leaving a gap.
