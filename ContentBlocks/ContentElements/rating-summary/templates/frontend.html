The stars are Atom/Rating, drawn from the score, so 4.6 renders as four and a bit rather than being rounded to a number nobody claimed; a German "4,6" is read as 4.6. They are silent for assistive technology because the score is printed right above them as text: five graphics announcing themselves would say less, not more. The score is two Text atoms rather than a Stat: a Stat is a description list, and the score has no term of its own — the heading above says what it rates.
