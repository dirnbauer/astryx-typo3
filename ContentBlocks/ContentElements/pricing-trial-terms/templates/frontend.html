A description list, because that is the shape of the content: each term is a question about the trial and each value is its answer. It also means a reader copying the panel into a mail to a colleague still gets pairs rather than two disconnected columns of words. The clarification is a second value on the same term rather than a paragraph of its own, so a term with a caveat still reads as one answer.
