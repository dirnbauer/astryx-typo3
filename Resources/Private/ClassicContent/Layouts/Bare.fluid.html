The frame for a plugin that brings its own band. EXT:solr's templates in this theme (Resources/Private/Solr) already open an Astryx Section and Container of their own — the results page needs its two-column grid at the extra-wide measure, not the content column. Putting them inside Default's section as well would nest a band in a band and double both the vertical rhythm and the gutters, so this layout adds nothing but the anchor every content element carries.
