The frame for content somebody else renders: every Extbase plugin CType (core registers them with templateName = Generic and the plugin at `.20`), among them the frontend login and the form framework's form. This template owns the band, the element's heading and the anchor; the plugin's own templates own everything inside and should not open a Section or Container of their own. `{content}` wins when a TypoScript setup has rendered the output already, exactly as in fluid_styled_content. A record from another table (a news record handed to lib.contentElement) has no plugin to call and renders its heading and body text instead. {content -> f:format.raw()}
