The pictures and films of Image, Text & Images and Text & Media: one Astryx Figure per file, its caption the file's own description. f:media rather than f:image, because a Text & Media element takes a YouTube or MP4 asset as readily as a photograph and f:image cannot draw either. It also supplies the alt text and title from the file reference, which is where an editor sets them. The column count is the editor's "Number of columns", but never more than there are files: two columns for a single picture would leave it at half width beside nothing.
