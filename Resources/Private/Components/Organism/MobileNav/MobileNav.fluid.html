MobileNav — the navigation drawer that slides in over a narrow page. A native `dialog` opened modally, which is what makes it a drawer rather than a panel: the top layer, the backdrop, the focus containment, Escape and the inertness of everything behind it are all the browser's. A div with a transform gets none of those, and the focus trap alone is a week of script that is never quite right. The one thing the platform will not do is open it, so the trigger calls `dialog.showModal()` — the existing `data-g-dialog-open=""` hook in astryx.js, which takes the id this component is given; the close control here uses that script's companion `data-g-dialog-close`. No new JavaScript, and `id` is required because both hooks find the dialog by it. The drawer transform is on the inner pane rather than on the dialog: the dialog itself must stay put and full-screen so that its backdrop covers the page, and `[open]` on the parent is what slides the pane in. The pane is also where the scrolling is — the dialog blocks touch panning so the page behind cannot be pulled, and the content region opts vertical panning back in for itself. `ariaLabel` is written twice, on the dialog and on the `nav` inside it, and that is deliberate. The dialog's name is what is announced at the moment it opens; the nav's is what appears in the landmark list once the visitor is inside. Leaving either off costs a name where a name is the only thing being said — "dialog", or "navigation", and nothing more.
The close control appears only once it has been given a name. An icon-only button with nothing to announce is read out as "button", and in a modal that is the way out.
