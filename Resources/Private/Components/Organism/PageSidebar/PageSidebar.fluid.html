PageSidebar — a content page's body when it has a column of sibling links beside it. The main content comes first in the source and the navigation follows it, and the grid is what moves the second beside the first. That order is the reading order for anyone not seeing the layout, and it is the right one: a visitor who arrived at this page wants the page, not the list of the pages around it. It is a `nav` landmark rather than a second content column, which is why `ariaLabel` matters — a page with a main navigation and this one has two nav landmarks, and a screen reader's landmark list is useless when both are called "navigation". The links reuse `astryx-nav__*`, the same recipe as the header's, because they are the same object: a list of pages, one of which is the one you are on. `aria-current="page"` is what says which, and the stylesheet paints it from that attribute rather than from a class, so the state cannot be drawn without also being announced. Like PageHeader, this sits inside a Container rather than being one. A component renders one root class and the measure is the Container's; nothing moves on screen, because a max-width and a grid do not interact.
