LayoutHeader — the top region of an application shell: the bar that holds a TopNav, and nothing else. A `header` element rather than a div, and that is the whole reason this component exists. At the top of a document `header` is the banner landmark, which is what lets a screen reader user jump to the site's controls and back out of them; a div would give them a bar they can only reach by tabbing through it. Everything about how the bar looks belongs to what is put inside it — this is a region, not a design. One per document. A `header` nested inside an `article` or a `section` is not a banner at all but that section's own introduction, so a second one of these further down the page silently stops being the landmark it claims to be.
