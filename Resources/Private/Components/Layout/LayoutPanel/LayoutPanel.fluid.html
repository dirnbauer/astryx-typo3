LayoutPanel — the column beside the content in an application shell: the one a SideNav, a filter rail or an inspector sits in. It owns the side of the shell it is on and its own scrolling, and nothing else: no width, because what it holds brings its own (a SideNav is 16rem expanded and a rail when collapsed, and a panel that also declared a width would fight it), and no background, because `inherit` lets the shell decide what the whole column is painted on. A plain `div` by default, and that is the part worth explaining. A panel holding a SideNav must not be an `aside`: the nav inside it is already a landmark, and wrapping it in a complementary one means a screen reader's landmark list offers two entries for one rail. Pass `as="aside"` only when the panel's contents are not a landmark of their own — an inspector, a filter column, a preview pane — and then name it with `ariaLabel`, because an unnamed complementary region announces as "complementary" and nothing more.
