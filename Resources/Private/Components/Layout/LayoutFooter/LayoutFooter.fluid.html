LayoutFooter — the bottom region of an application shell: a status bar, a row of secondary actions, the small print under a tool. A `footer` element, which at the top level of a document is the contentinfo landmark. That is the point of the component, the same way LayoutHeader's point is the banner: the region is reachable by name instead of only by tabbing to the end of the page. It is deliberately not SiteFooter. SiteFooter is a marketing page's foot — columns of links, a newsletter form, a legal row — and it carries its own layout and spacing. This is a strip at the bottom of an app shell with no opinion at all about what goes in it.
