Center — one thing in the middle of the space it has. Two kinds of centring that look alike and are not: the default is a flex box that centres a child on both axes, which is what a play button over a poster frame needs, while `variant="text"` centres the text inside the box and the box itself in its parent, which is what a centred heading and lead paragraph need. Using the first where the second was meant collapses the children onto one line, so the distinction is worth the argument.
