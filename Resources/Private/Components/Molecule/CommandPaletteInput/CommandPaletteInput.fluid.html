CommandPaletteInput — the query row at the top of a command palette: a loupe, the field, and nothing else. The field is a bare `input` rather than Molecule/TextInput, and the reason is the ARIA rather than the styling. A combobox carries role="combobox", aria-autocomplete, aria-controls, aria-expanded and aria-activedescendant; TextInput declares none of them, Fluid drops any attribute a component has not declared, and a combobox missing aria-controls is a combobox a screen reader cannot follow. Borrowing the input and losing half its contract would have been worse than writing eight lines of input here. Two of those five are the server's and three are not. role="combobox", aria-autocomplete="list" and aria-controls describe how the widget is built and never change, so they are written outright. aria-expanded and aria-activedescendant describe what the widget is doing right now — they are declared as arguments with the values a closed, unhighlighted palette has, and a script is what keeps them honest afterwards. An aria-expanded that is written once and never updated is worse than none: it tells a screen reader the menu is shut while the reader is looking at it. The row has no border of its own and the field has no box: inside a palette the dialog's own edge is the frame, and a field drawn inside a frame inside a frame is what made the first version look like a form in a modal rather than a palette. The label is visually hidden rather than absent. The placeholder disappears the moment the visitor types, which leaves a voice-control user with nothing to say the field's name.
