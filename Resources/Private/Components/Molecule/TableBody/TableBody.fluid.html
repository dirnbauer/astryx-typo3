TableBody — a table's `tbody`. The counterpart to TableHeader, and it does carry a rule of its own: the last row in the body drops its bottom border, because that line would otherwise sit under the table rather than between two cells and read as a stray rule. The stylesheet spells that as `.astryx-table-body .astryx-table-row:last-child`, so a body of TableRows gets it and a `tbody` of bare `tr`s gets it from the `:where(tbody …)` selector beside it. Both work; do not mix them in one table. A table may have more than one `tbody` — that is how a long table is grouped by section — and nothing here prevents it. The last-row rule then applies per body, which is the right answer: each group's final row ends its group.
