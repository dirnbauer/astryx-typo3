Carousel — a row that scrolls sideways and snaps. The scrolling, the snapping, the drag and the momentum are CSS and the browser; the two arrows are an enhancement on top of a control that already works, and astryx.js disables them at either end so they never lie about what they will do. `data-g-carousel` is therefore emitted unconditionally: with no arrows in the markup the script finds nothing to wire, and a rail that later grows a pair of them needs no change here. The track and the arrows are the caller's. The track carries the element's own class beside `astryx-carousel-track`, and every rail labels it differently — some by a heading already on the page, some by a phrase of their own — so the scroll region is written where that decision is made. `ariaLabel` decides the element, as it does for Toolbar: a rail of links that moves a visitor around the site is navigation and needs a named `nav` landmark; an unnamed rail of cards is a plain `div`.
