CarouselItem — one cell of a Carousel's track. A list item, because a rail is a list of things and a reader who cannot see it should be told how many there are and be able to step through them. The scroll snapping is the item's own — the stylesheet gives it the snap alignment and the width that makes the next card peek past the edge — which is why the class has to be on the cell rather than inferred by the track. `:focus-within` on this element is what scrolls a cell into view when a keyboard reaches a link inside it, so a rail stays operable without the arrows. Nothing here needs scripting.
