InputGroup — an input welded to whatever sits either side of it: a leading icon, a trailing submit button. The stylesheet gives every direct child `flex: 1`, which is right for the field and wrong for a loupe or a button; both search forms correct it in their own sheet. Nothing here can make that call, because nothing here knows which child is the field — so the group stays a plain row and the correction stays where the knowledge is. The `addon` slot is the segment that is not a control: a leading loupe, a currency sign, a fixed URL prefix. It is rendered first and wrapped in `astryx-input-group-text`, which is the class the stylesheet paints that segment with and the reason it is a slot rather than something the caller writes — the group owns its own seams, and a caller that spells the wrapper by hand is one rename away from an unstyled box. One trap comes with the named slots, and it is Fluid's rather than this component's: a call that contains even one `` stops collecting its loose children as the default slot. Content meant for `` has to be spelled `` too, or it renders as nothing and nothing warns you.
