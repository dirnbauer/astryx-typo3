ChatComposerDrawer — the tray of extra controls the composer keeps folded away: attachments, a model picker, whatever a site hangs off a message. It composes Molecule/Collapsible, so it is a native `details` and the open state, the keyboard behaviour and the printer expanding it are the browser's. Nothing here toggles anything, and the tray keeps working with the stylesheet stripped. `details` nests inside a `form` without complaint, and the controls in the tray are submitted with the message whether the tray is open or closed — `open` is a presentation state and not a disabled state, which is exactly what a folded-away tray of options should be. Hiding the controls with `display: none` instead, which is what a scripted drawer usually does, would leave them in the form but out of the accessibility tree, and a required control in there would block submission with nothing to point at. The tray opens downward. Upstream animates it upward over the transcript, which needs the panel positioned out of flow and the summary told which way to point; folded into the composer's own column it costs nothing and pushes nothing off screen. {label}
