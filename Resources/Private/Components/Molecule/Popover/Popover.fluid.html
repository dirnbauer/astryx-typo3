Popover — the surface a menu, a filter list or a small form opens onto. The surface only: rounding, the popover background token and a low shadow. Where it opens and what opens it are the caller's, because the theme has two ways of answering that and both are right. A native `[popover]` with an invoker gets anchor positioning and light dismiss from the browser; a `
` disclosure with the panel positioned in its own stylesheet gets Escape and the open state from the browser instead. Baking either one in here would rule the other out. The padded inner box is always rendered rather than being a second component. A popover with no padding is a shadow drawn tight against its own text, and every caller would write the same wrapper back by hand.
