Give it an `ariaLabelledby` pointing at its own title where the landmark deserves a name. `data-status` is upstream's own attribute name since Astryx v0.6.0. The tinted background carries the meaning on its own, so a banner never depends on an icon to say whether it is a warning: the icon slot is decoration and the status is the message. The content wrapper appears only when this component renders the title. `astryx-banner-content` exists to hold the title and the body together as one column against the icon and the dismiss button on either side; with no title there is nothing to group, and wrapping a caller's own markup in it would swallow an icon they placed beside the text. An icon-only control needs an accessible name whatever the caller does, so the translated "Close" stands in when no label is passed.
