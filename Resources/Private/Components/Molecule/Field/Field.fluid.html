Field — one labelled control, together with its hint and its validation message. The control arrives through the slot, and a Fluid component cannot reach into slotted content to annotate it. So the wiring is made explicit rather than magical: `inputId` names the control, the hint and the message take ids derived from it, and the caller hands those ids straight back to the input as `describedBy`. TextInput turns `status="error"` into aria-invalid at its own end. Deriving both ids from one argument is what keeps the two halves of the pair in step when a field is copied. The message is a polite live region: a validation error that appears after the page has settled has to be said, not merely painted red.
