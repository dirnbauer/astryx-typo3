TopNavMenu — a bar entry that opens a small panel of links under itself. A native popover, opened by `popovertarget`: the top layer, Escape, the click outside and the anchoring to the invoker are all the browser's, and the trigger's expanded state is the browser's too. Nothing here writes `aria-expanded` — a server-rendered `false` on a menu the visitor has just opened is worse than no attribute, and the stylesheet reads the panel's `:popover-open` instead, including for the tint and the chevron it turns. That reading is `:has(+ [popover]:popover-open)`, which holds only while the trigger and the panel are siblings — so the root here is a wrapper around the pair rather than the trigger alone. It is `display: contents`, so the bar lays out the button and not a box around it. The panel's contents are the caller's, through the `menu` slot: a column of TopNavMegaMenuItems for a menu with descriptions, or Items for a plain list of links. This component decides nothing about them beyond the surface they sit on.
