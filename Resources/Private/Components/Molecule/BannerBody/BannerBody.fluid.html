BannerBody — the message of a Banner, under its title. It carries no type of its own: the banner already sets the size and the colour of everything in it, and the only thing left to say is that this paragraph does not add a margin the tinted box would then have to absorb. `as` is here because the message is sometimes one sentence and sometimes a field of editor rich text. A paragraph cannot legally hold the headings and lists that come out of the second, so a body carrying prose is a `div`. That is a question about the content, not about the look, which is why it is an argument rather than two components.
