CollapsibleGroup — several Collapsibles stacked as one panel, hairlines between them and no edges of its own. Mutual exclusivity is the `name` attribute on the disclosures themselves, not something this element can impose: a component cannot reach into its slot and set an attribute on what a caller put there. So `name` is passed to each Collapsible as well, and is recorded here as `data-name` so that the group and its rows can be seen to agree. Key it to the record's uid, or two accordions on one page will close each other's rows. By default the group draws no border round itself. Whatever wraps it — a card, a section — owns its outer edge, and a border here would double up with it. `dividers="all"` is for the group that stands in open page with nothing round it: a disclosure list under a heading, where the rules between the rows end at the first and the last row and the list has no top or bottom. It adds a rule above the first row and below the last, in the same hairline as the ones between.
