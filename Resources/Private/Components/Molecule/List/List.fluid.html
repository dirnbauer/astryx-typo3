    rather than a
      , because a numbered list is an ordered list and the number is the meaning, not the decoration. `step` draws the number in the tinted tile FeatureIcon uses for its numerals, for a short process told as a list: how to join, how a referral works. Every other marker leaves the list unordered. The markers themselves are drawn by the CSS rather than left to `list-style-type`, so that a bullet can take the text colour and line up with the first line of an item that wraps. `dividers` and `density` are separate on purpose: a rule between rows replaces the gap entirely, and the padding that gives that rule its air is the density's job. `columns` (2 to 4) lays the rows out in columns, a ceiling as on Grid: a directory of people, a list of alumni. With dividers every row keeps its rule there, because no rule can know which rows end the last line. `orientation="horizontal"` runs the items along a line and wraps them: the legal links under a footer, a row of languages, a strip of short facts. `dividers` then draws a short upright rule between two items instead of a line under each — drawn, not typed, so it never lands in a copied line or in what a screen reader reads out — and drops it below 480px, where the row wraps and a leading rule would mark the start of a line rather than a boundary. In a horizontal list the dividers take the place of a marker. `gap` (1 to 8, a step of the spacing scale) sets the space between items: between the rows of a vertical list, between the items of a horizontal one. `size="sm"` sets the items in the supporting text size: a compact strip of jump links or facts beside running text. The default is body text. `facetName` and `facetLabel` are EXT:solr's own markup contract for a facet option list, read back by its show-all and ajaxify scripts. They are declared here rather than written onto a hand-rolled `ul` in the search partial because the alternative is a list that no longer composes this component and therefore no longer gets its rhythm — and a facet list is still a list. They render nothing when unset.
