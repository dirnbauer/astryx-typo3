List — a vertical run of rows: bullets, steps, links, Items. `marker="decimal"` renders an
    rather than a
      , because a numbered list is an ordered list and the number is the meaning, not the decoration. Every other marker leaves the list unordered. The markers themselves are drawn by the CSS rather than left to `list-style-type`, so that a bullet can take the text colour and line up with the first line of an item that wraps. `dividers` and `density` are separate on purpose: a rule between rows replaces the gap entirely, and the padding that gives that rule its air is the density's job.
