    rather than a

The markers themselves are drawn by the CSS rather than left to `list-style-type`, so that a bullet can take the text colour and line up with the first line of an item that wraps. `dividers` and `density` are separate on purpose: a rule between rows replaces the gap entirely, and the padding that gives that rule its air is the density's job. `facetName` and `facetLabel` are EXT:solr's own markup contract for a facet option list, read back by its show-all and ajaxify scripts. They are declared here rather than written onto a hand-rolled `ul` in the search partial because the alternative is a list that no longer composes this component and therefore no longer gets its rhythm — and a facet list is still a list. They render nothing when unset.
