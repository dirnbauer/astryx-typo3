TopNavMegaMenu — the bar entry that opens the wide panel: columns of described links with a featured card beside them. The same machinery as TopNavMenu — a native popover opened by `popovertarget`, with the top layer, Escape, the outside click and the anchoring all coming from the browser, and no `aria-expanded` written by a server that cannot know it. It is a component of its own rather than a `variant` because the panel is a different thing: a wrapping row of columns up to 60rem wide, not a list. The trigger's classes differ too, which is what lets a theme retune one kind of menu without touching the other. The root is the wrapper around trigger and panel, because the stylesheet's `:has(+ [popover]:popover-open)` — the tint and the turned chevron — only holds while they are siblings. `display: contents` keeps the wrapper out of the bar's layout. The panel lays its children out itself, in a wrapping flex row: give it TopNavMegaMenuItems and a TopNavMegaMenuFeaturedCard and they will sit in columns. There is no column component in between, because the wrap is what decides where the columns break and a wrapper would freeze it.
