Tooltip — the small panel that explains the word or the glyph it is attached to. It renders the whole arrangement: the anchor, the trigger and the tip. It used to render the tip alone and leave the anchor to the caller, on the grounds that only the caller has the trigger — but callers are templates, templates may not write `astryx-` classes, and the reveal the stylesheet implements hangs entirely off `.astryx-tooltip-anchor`. So it was a mechanism nothing could reach. The reveal is the anchor's `:hover` *and* its `:focus-within`, which is why the trigger is focusable: a tip that answered only the pointer is a tip a keyboard can never bring up, and `:focus-within` needs something inside the anchor that can take focus. That is also why a control does not go in the trigger slot — put a term, an abbreviation or an icon there. A tip that explains a button belongs on the button, where `aria-describedby` reaches it directly; wrapping the button here would add a tab stop in front of it and describe the wrapper instead. `id` is required rather than optional, because being near something is not how a tip gets announced: the trigger has to point at it, and an empty id points at nothing.
