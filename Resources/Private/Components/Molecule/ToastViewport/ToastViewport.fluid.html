ToastViewport — the fixed strip in a corner of the page that Toasts sit in. Rendered once, by the page chrome, holding none or many of them. That is the whole reason it is a component and not something each toast brings with it: both toast roles announce their element when it *appears*, so a message has to arrive inside a region that was already on the page. It is a plain fixed strip rather than the manual popover it was drawn as. A manual popover is display:none until something calls showPopover(), and this theme ships no script to call it — the region and every toast in it would simply never be on screen. What that costs is the top layer: toasts stack under an open `dialog` instead of over it. Not appearing at all is the worse of the two. No `aria-live` here. Each Toast carries its own `role="status"` or `role="alert"`, and a live region wrapped around live regions is one announcement made twice.
