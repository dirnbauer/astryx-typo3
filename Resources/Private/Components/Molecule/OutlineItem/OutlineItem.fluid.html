OutlineItem — one line of an Outline's "on this page" rail. The hairline the rail is drawn against belongs to Outline; the segment of it that the current entry paints belongs here, and the stylesheet takes it from `aria-current` rather than from a class. So marking an entry as current is one statement that both a screen reader and the marker read, and there is no second, visual-only spelling of it to fall out of step. `level` indents a nested heading, as a plain number. It reaches the markup as `data-level`, like every other modifier in this theme — the stylesheet was written against `.astryx-outline-item.level-2` until 2.0 and now reads `[data-level="2"]`. Put the text in a `span`: the stylesheet truncates `.astryx-outline-item > span` at one line, and a bare text node has nothing to truncate.
