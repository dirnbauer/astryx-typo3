ChatMessageBubble — the surface a turn's words sit on. It does not say whose turn it is. The ChatMessage around it already carries `data-role`, and the stylesheet paints the bubble from that ancestor, so a bubble cannot be tinted as the user's while sitting inside an assistant's turn. Restating the role here would be a second copy of one fact and the two would eventually disagree. `variant="plain"` drops the fill and the padding for the turns that are not speech — a long answer with its own headings, a table, an error report. Those read worse boxed, and the bubble was never what made them a turn; the list item was. No `aria-label`, no role, no landmark. It is the box around text that has already been announced, and an extra name on it would make a screen reader say the turn twice.
