EmptyState — what a list says when it has nothing in it. An empty state that only reports "0 results" leaves a visitor with no move to make, so this is shaped around saying what is missing and what to try next: an icon, a line naming what was looked for, and a hint. All three go in the one slot, without a wrapper of their own. The stylesheet sizes and tints the icon through `astryx-empty-state > svg`, so the icon has to be a direct child — putting it in a region of its own would leave it at whatever size it was drawn.
