OverflowList — a row of small items that refuses to grow: logos, payment marks, tags. Whatever does not fit is clipped, or, with `wrap="multi-row"`, allowed onto a second line. A list rather than a row of `div`s, because "eleven customer logos" is something a screen reader should be able to count and skip. It therefore needs a name: `labelledBy` points at a heading or a note already on the page, which is what the 1.x elements do, and `ariaLabel` is for the case where there is no such text to point at.
