TextInput — a single-line control, rendered as the bare `input` the stylesheet is written against rather than wrapped in a shell of its own. Three of its attributes are HTML booleans, and `disabled=""` is a disabled field: they genuinely cannot be emitted empty. Spelling each one as a two-branch f:if the way Button does would mean eight copies of the same tag for three independent flags, so the attribute name itself is what the inline f:if returns — the name, or nothing at all. `status="error"` also renders aria-invalid. The red border is a colour, and a colour on its own is not a message.
