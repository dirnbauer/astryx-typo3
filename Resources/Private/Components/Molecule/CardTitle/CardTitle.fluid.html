CardTitle — the heading of a card. `level` picks the element and nothing else. A card is almost never the top of a page's outline, so the default is h3: a grid of cards under one h2 section heading then reads as a flat list of siblings rather than as a nest of sections. Callers that place a card under a deeper heading say so with level="4" instead of leaving the outline wrong. No data attribute goes with it. The contract gives CardTitle no modifiers, and the card's own type scale is what sets the size; a title that needs a different size is an Atom/Heading, not a card title. The id branch exists because `id=""` is not a conforming HTML id, and a card title is the natural target of an aria-labelledby on whatever region the card sits in.
