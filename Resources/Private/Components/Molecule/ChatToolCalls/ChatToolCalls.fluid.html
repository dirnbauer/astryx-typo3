ChatToolCalls — what the assistant did before it answered, one disclosure per call. Each call composes Molecule/Collapsible rather than writing a second `details`. The open state, the keyboard behaviour, find-in-page reaching text inside a closed panel and the printer expanding every panel are all the browser's, and Collapsible already has them plus the exclusivity grouping. What this file adds is the row: the tool's name, its outcome and the payload. An `ol`, because the calls ran in an order and the third one usually only makes sense after the second. It carries `role="list"` for the reason ChatMessageList does: the stylesheet takes the markers off, and Safari drops list semantics from an unmarkered list. The disclosures are closed by default — a transcript is read for the answer, and six expanded argument dumps between the question and the reply bury it. A call is a map with `name`, optionally `status` and `statusLabel`, and optionally `detail`. The outcome needs both halves: `status` is the attribute the tint hangs off, `statusLabel` is the word a reader actually gets, and the badge is rendered only when the words are there — a green pill with nothing in it says nothing to anyone who cannot see green. `group` puts the disclosures in one exclusivity group so opening a call closes the last one. Left unset they open independently, which is the right default when a reader is comparing two calls. With no `calls` the slot is rendered instead, for the callers that have already built their own rows.
