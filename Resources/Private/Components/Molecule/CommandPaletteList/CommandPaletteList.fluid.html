CommandPaletteList — the scrolling listbox a command palette's input points at. `id` has no default and is what makes the widget work: the input's aria-controls names this element, and a listbox nothing points at is a list a screen reader never learns is connected to the field. The two ids are passed rather than derived because the palette owns both and can keep them in step, while a component cannot reach into a sibling to read one. It is named by the same words as the field. A listbox with no accessible name is announced as "list box" and leaves the reader to guess whether they are in the results or somewhere else; naming it after the field they are typing in is the shortest true answer. Scrolling lives here and not on the dialog. The palette's footer of keyboard hints has to stay in view while the matches move under it — the first version scrolled the whole dialog and the hints left the screen exactly when a visitor navigating by keyboard needed them.
