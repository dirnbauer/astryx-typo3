TopNavMegaMenuFeaturedCard — the promoted block at the end of a mega menu: an image over a short pitch, usually linking to one thing the site wants read next. A link when it has an href and a plain block when it has not, because a card that goes nowhere is a panel of text and should not be announced as a link or sit in the tab order. Its own component rather than Card, even though both are a picture over some words: this one's proportions come from the menu panel it lives in — a 12rem column that flexes with its neighbours, an image cropped to a fixed strip — and a Card in that slot brings a surface, a border and a padding scale that belong to the page. The image is a direct child and the text is in the block beside it, because that is how the stylesheet tells them apart: `> img` is bled to the card's edges, `> :not(img)` is the padded part. Nothing may come between them. `imageAlt` defaults to empty, which is the right answer when the card's own heading already says what the picture shows — an alt repeating the title is read out twice. Where the picture carries information the words do not, say so by passing one.
