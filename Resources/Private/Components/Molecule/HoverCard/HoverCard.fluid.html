HoverCard — the preview panel that opens from a name or a link. Tooltip's bigger sibling, and it renders the panel and nothing else for the same reason: the wrapper that reveals it, `astryx-hover-card-anchor`, has to go around the trigger, and only the caller has the trigger. The stylesheet opens the card from that wrapper's `:hover` *and* its `:focus-within`, so pointer and keyboard get it from one rule. It is not `role="tooltip"`. A tooltip may not contain anything interactive, and the whole point of a hover card is that it holds an avatar, a line of prose and a link to the profile — content a reader has to be able to move into. It has no role at all, therefore, and is announced because the trigger points at it with `aria-describedby`. That reference is why `id` is required rather than optional: a card nothing points at is a card a screen reader never mentions. `popover` chooses between the two mechanisms the stylesheet supports. As a popover it gets the top layer, light dismiss and anchor positioning, and it needs a `popovertarget` button to open it — a hover card opened by a click, which is the right answer on a touch screen. Without it, it is a plain panel the anchor reveals on hover or focus, which costs no interaction at all. Neither is the default for both cases, so the caller says which. The two branches exist because `popover=""` is not an absent popover — the empty value is the `auto` state, so the attribute is written or the tag is written without it.
