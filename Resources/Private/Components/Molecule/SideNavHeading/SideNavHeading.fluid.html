SideNavHeading — what the rail is called, at the top of a SideNav: a mark, a title, and up to two lines of smaller text around it. The same three shapes as TopNavHeading and for the same reason: an href makes it a link, `interactive` without an href makes it the button that opens a switcher, and neither makes it a plain `div` that stays out of the tab order. It is a separate component rather than an argument on that one because the two carry different classes, and a theme that retunes the rail without touching the bar — which is most of them — has to be able to reach one and not the other. `.astryx-side-nav-heading-text` is added by this file's stylesheet rather than inherited from the bar's: the heading is a flex row, so a title with a sub under it needs a column wrapper, and only the TopNav side of the 2.0 stylesheet ever got one. In the collapsed rail the heading keeps its mark and loses nothing else — the title is not hidden here the way item labels are, so a rail that collapses should be given a heading that is mostly its NavIcon. {f:if(condition: href, then: 'link', else: '{f:if(condition: interactive, then: \'button\', else: \'plain\')}')} {super}{title}{sub} `popovertarget` is the browser's own invoker — open, toggle, Escape and click-outside without a line of script. The panel it names is the caller's, because what a project switcher lists is not something the rail's title can know.
