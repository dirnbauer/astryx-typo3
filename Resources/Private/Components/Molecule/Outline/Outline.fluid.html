Outline — the "on this page" rail: one line per heading, against a hairline that the current entry paints its own segment of. A `div` wrapping the caller's list, not a `nav` wrapping a list of its own. The rail is placed inside a landmark that already names it — the outline rail element puts it in a `nav` labelled with the page's own heading — and a second, identically named navigation landmark inside the first would offer the same rail twice. Whoever places this owns the landmark and the list; this owns the rail and the hairline behind it. No entry marks itself as active. Knowing which section a reader is looking at is a scroll spy, a scroll spy is a script, and the marker in the stylesheet is driven by aria-current on whichever entry the caller decides is the current one.
