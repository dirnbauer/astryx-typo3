ChatLayoutScrollButton — the control that returns to the newest message. It is a link to an anchor, not a button, and that is the only way a scroll-to-bottom can work here. Scrolling a box is `scrollTo()`, which is script; following a fragment link is the browser, and the browser will scroll the nearest scrolling ancestor of the target on its own. The target is the empty marker ChatLayout renders at the foot of the transcript, so `target` defaults to that component's default `endId` and the two agree without a caller naming both. `scroll-behavior: smooth` on the transcript makes the jump glide, and prefers-reduced-motion turns that off. Because it navigates it is an `a` rather than a `button`, so it opens in a new tab on a middle click and shows a real target in the status bar, both of which a scripted scroll button silently breaks. What it cannot do is hide itself when the transcript is already at the bottom, which is what upstream does — whether a box is scrolled is not something a selector can ask today outside a scroll-state container query, and this theme cannot rely on that yet. So it is always rendered. A control that is visible and works is the better half of that trade. `ariaLabel` carries the whole meaning; the arrow is drawn `aria-hidden`.
