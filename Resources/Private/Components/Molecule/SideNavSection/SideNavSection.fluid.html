SideNavSection — one labelled group of entries in a SideNav: "Workspace", "Recent", "Settings". The entries are a real list. A rail is a list of destinations, and a screen reader announcing "list, six items" before reading them is the difference between knowing how much is there and finding out by arrowing to the end. The `ul` is this component's, so that SideNavItem can be an `li` without every caller having to remember to wrap them. The section title is a `div`, not a heading. It labels the list rather than starting a piece of content, and a rail full of h3s injects a whole tier into the document outline that the page itself never asked for. Passing `id` is what makes the label reach assistive technology: the list points at it with aria-labelledby and the group is then announced by name. Without an id the title is still read in order, just not attached to the list — which is why the argument exists at all. In the collapsed rail the title is hidden and the rows shrink to their icons. The title is clipped rather than removed, so the label the list points at still exists.
