ItemLabel — the line of an Item that names what the row is. `data-truncate="truncate"` clips the label to one line with an ellipsis. It is opt-in rather than the default because a clipped label is a label a visitor cannot finish reading, and in most lists wrapping onto a second line is the better failure. `size` and `weight` are for the rows whose label is a title rather than a name: a job opening, a document, a course. `size="lg"` sets it at the large text size, `weight="medium"` or `"semibold"` gives it a title's weight; together they let a row of openings read as a list of headlines without the label becoming a heading — the row is still one item of a list, and its outline belongs to whatever heads the list.
