Stepper — the progress a visitor has made through a sequence: a checkout, a multi-page form, an onboarding path. An ordered list, because the steps are in an order and that order is the whole meaning; rendering them as `div`s would leave a screen reader with four unrelated words. Which step is current is said by the step itself rather than by this element, so a stepper split across two columns or re-rendered on the server still agrees with itself. Promoted to @astryxdesign/core at v0.6.0, which is where its data-status vocabulary — complete, current, upcoming — comes from.
