BannerContent — the middle column of a Banner: everything between the icon and the dismiss control, held together as one block. It exists because a banner is a three-part row and only the middle part wraps. `flex: 1 1 auto` with `min-inline-size: 0` is what lets a long message shrink instead of pushing the close button off the end, and that only works on an element that holds the whole message rather than on each paragraph of it. Banner writes one of these itself for the simple case — a title and a line of text passed as `title` and a slot. This is the other case: a heading with a badge beside it, a body, a row of links and a footnote, in an order the element decides. It is CardTitle's relationship to Card, and the same reason: a component that knows what its content is made of is a component every new arrangement has to be added to.
