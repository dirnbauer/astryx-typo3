Card — the surface a self-contained piece of content sits on. It owns the box and nothing else. The title, the prose and the action row are CardTitle, CardBody and CardFooter, so a card can be assembled in any order and a caller can leave any of the three out without this component having to know what a card is made of. The media region is a slot of its own rather than simply the first thing in the default slot, because `data-media="top"` bleeds the media out to the card's padding with a negative margin on the first child. That rule needs a first child it can rely on, and a default slot cannot promise one.
An unfilled slot renders as null, so the media region costs nothing when a caller has no media to give it.
