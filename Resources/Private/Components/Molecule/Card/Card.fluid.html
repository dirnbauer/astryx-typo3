Card — the surface a self-contained piece of content sits on. It owns the box and nothing else. The title, the prose and the action row are CardTitle, CardBody and CardFooter, so a card can be assembled in any order and a caller can leave any of the three out without this component having to know what a card is made of. The media region is a slot of its own rather than simply the first thing in the default slot, because `data-media="top"` bleeds the media out to the card's padding with a negative margin on the first child. That rule needs a first child it can rely on, and a default slot cannot promise one. `clickable` puts the whole card's link affordance on the card itself rather than in a ClickableCard around it, and that is a deliberate exception to one component, one root class. The affordance is not a box: it is a position, a hover lift and the stretched pseudo-element that grows the card's own link over the surface. Wrapped in a div of its own, the wrapper becomes the grid item and the card inside it stops stretching, so a row of cards loses its equal heights — which is why ClickableCard, a year on, has no call site at all. ClickableCard stays for the rows that are not cards. `featured` is the editor's "this is the plan we recommend", which a plan card's own stylesheet keys off; `as` exists only so that a card which is a self-contained piece of writing can say so with an `article`. {f:if(condition: clickable, then: 'astryx-clickable-card')}
An unfilled slot renders as null, so the media region costs nothing when a caller has no media to give it.
