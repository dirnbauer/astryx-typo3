Step — one stage of a Stepper. Three states, upstream's own since v0.6.0: complete, current, upcoming. The current one carries aria-current="step" as well as the data attribute, because the attribute is for the stylesheet and aria-current is for the reader who cannot see it. The other two spell the state out in `statusLabel`, rendered visually hidden — a tick and a hollow circle are a distinction only a sighted reader can make. The indicator takes upstream's colour vocabulary rather than the step's own: the compiled themes tint `astryx-step-indicator` through data-status="accent" and data-status="success", so "current" and "complete" are translated on the way in. What the circle holds is a slot, because whether that is a number, a tick or nothing is a decision about the sequence and not about the step.
