CommandPaletteFooter — the strip of keyboard hints along the bottom of a palette: arrows to move, Return to run, Escape to leave. The hints are arguments rather than slotted markup, and that is the point of the component. A hint is a Kbd or two followed by the words that say what the key does, and a template cannot write `astryx-kbd-group` itself — the class belongs to the design system. Leaving the arrangement to call sites would have meant either an exception in the class rule or four call sites each inventing their own spacing. Each hint appears only once it has words. A row of keys with nothing beside them is decoration: the glyph that a screen reader reads as "up arrow" means nothing on its own, and the words are what make the pair a sentence. That is also why the keys are not aria-hidden — read together they say "up arrow down arrow to navigate", which is the whole hint. The keys themselves are arguments too, because they are not universal: Escape is labelled Esc in most places and Escape in some, and a palette bound to a different key than Return has to be able to say so. The slot is for a hint this component does not know about, and comes last so the three standard ones keep their order.
