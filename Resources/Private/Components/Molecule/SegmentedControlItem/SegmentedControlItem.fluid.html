SegmentedControlItem — one segment of a SegmentedControl's rail. Which element is rendered follows Button's rule rather than being a free choice: `parameter` is a TYPO3 link field and becomes a TypoLink, `href` is a URL the template already resolved, and a segment that navigates is an anchor either way. A segment that toggles something without leaving the page is a `button`, and that is what neither argument means. The size reaches the markup as `data-size`, like every other modifier in this theme; the stylesheet was written against `.astryx-segmented-control-item.sm` until 2.0 and reads `[data-size="sm"]` now. Passing nothing leaves the segment at its default height.
