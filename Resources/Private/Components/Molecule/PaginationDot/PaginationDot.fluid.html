PaginationDot — one dot of a PaginationDots row. An anchor to a fragment, not a button. Which view a slideshow is showing is then the browser's own business: the target scrolls into view, the address bar records it, the choice survives a reload and the back button undoes it, all without a line of script. That is the whole reason the dot is a link. A dot has no text of its own, so the slot is where its name goes — visually hidden, but the only thing a screen reader has to tell one dot from the next. Marking the current one is `aria-current="page"`, which the stylesheet paints from as well, so the filled dot and the announced position cannot disagree.
