ChatMessage — one turn of the conversation, as a list item in the transcript. An `li`, because ChatMessageList is an `ol` and a conversation's order is part of its meaning: the answer after the question is not the same document as the answer before it. Rendering a turn as a floating div would throw that away and leave a screen reader with no count either. `author` is required, and that is the whole accessibility argument of this component. Who said what is carried by a bubble's colour and by which edge it is pushed to, and neither of those reaches a screen reader, a reader in forced-colours mode or anyone who cannot tell the two tints apart. The name is therefore real text in the markup, always rendered, and the alignment in `data-role` is the decoration on top of it rather than the message itself. `data-role` is one of `user`, `assistant` or `system`. It is what the stylesheet aligns and tints by, and it is deliberately the only place the role is stated — the bubble inside reads it from this ancestor instead of being told a second time, because two copies of one fact drift. The avatar is a slot of its own and is drawn `aria-hidden`: it is a picture of the author whose name is already beside it, so announcing it would read the same turn's author twice.
