DropdownMenuSubMenu — a run of a menu that folds away behind its own label. This is the one part of the menu pattern the platform cannot express, and the component says so rather than faking it. A true submenu is a `menuitem` carrying `aria-haspopup="menu"` and an `aria-expanded` that flips as the panel opens and closes. Nothing but script flips it, and a `summary` — the only element that opens a panel on its own — may be a button and never a `menuitem`. So the choice was a `menuitem` with an `aria-expanded` that lies the moment anybody uses it, or a disclosure that tells the truth about what it is. It is the disclosure. `details` carries its own expanded state to the accessibility tree, works with scripting off, and survives find-in-page and printing. The panel inside is `role="group"` rather than a nested `role="menu"`, which keeps the rows inside part of the menu they were always part of — `group` is a role a `menu` may own, a nested `menu` under a button that is not a `menuitem` is not. The cost, stated plainly: the summary is announced as a collapsed button inside a menu rather than as a submenu item. That is the one place this family is not strictly conforming, and it buys an expander that actually expands. `open` renders the group already unfolded, for the submenu holding the row the visitor is currently on.
