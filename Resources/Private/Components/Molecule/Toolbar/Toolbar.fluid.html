Toolbar — a row with a start, a centre and an end: the bar above a list, a sticky call to action, a strip of page links. `ariaLabel` decides the element, the way `href` decides Button's. A row of links that moves a visitor around the site is navigation and belongs in a `nav`; a `nav` with no accessible name is worse than no landmark at all, because a screen reader then lists an anonymous region. So naming the row is exactly what makes it one, and an unnamed row stays a plain `div`. There is no `as` argument, because the two are never a free choice. The three regions — `astryx-toolbar-start`, `-center`, `-end` — are the caller's own elements rather than slots here. They carry the element's class alongside the toolbar's, every existing bar already writes them, and a component that emitted them unconditionally would double each one.
