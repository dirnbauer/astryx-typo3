TableFooter — a table's `tfoot`: the totals row, the sum, the "12 of 340". It is heavier than the body and separated by the emphasised border, because a footer that looked like one more row would be read as one more row, and a total is not a data point. Written after the body in the markup. `tfoot` used to have to come first and browsers still move it to the bottom either way, but the source order is what a screen reader and a plain-text rendering follow, and the total belongs after the numbers it totals.
