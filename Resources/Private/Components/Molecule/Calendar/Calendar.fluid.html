Calendar — a month, as a table of dates. Not a date picker. A picker is a popover, a roving grid and a script, and none of the three exist here; the control that asks a visitor for a day is DateInput, which hands the job to the browser's own picker. This is for showing a month — opening times, an event month, a room's bookings — where a `` is not a layout hack but the right element: seven columns with `
` is what lets a screen reader say "Wednesday 17" instead of reading a wall of numbers. Today is `aria-current="date"`. The accent ring says the same thing to everyone who can see it, which is the point of saying it twice. The month is built here from arrays rather than being assembled by the caller, and that is the layer rule rather than a preference: no template in this extension may write an Astryx class, so the day cells cannot be the caller's markup. `weeks` is a list of weeks, each a list of days, each day `{label, date, current, selected, disabled, outside}` — `label` the number to print, `date` its ISO form for the ``. A day with no `label` is a lead-in or trail-out cell and renders as an empty box. `weekdays` is seven `{short, long}` pairs: the column heads print the short form and carry the long one in an ``, so "Mo" stays two characters wide and is still read out as Monday. A disabled day is struck through as well as dimmed. Dimming on its own reads as "quiet", not as "not available".
