ListItem — one entry of a List: the `li` that carries the list's marker. It is deliberately thin, and the reason is Item. Item is a row — leading media, a label and a description, a trailing action, hover, pressed, selected, and an `href` that turns the whole row into a link. ListItem is a bullet point. Both render an `li`, so the choice is about what the entry is, not about what it looks like: a list of sentences takes ListItems, a list of people or search results or menu entries takes Items. The markers are the concrete difference. List draws them as pseudo-elements on its direct children, matching `li` as well as `.astryx-list-item`, so an Item dropped into a `marker="dot"` list gets a bullet in front of its avatar — which is why a marked list wants these and an interactive list wants Items. Nothing stops the two being mixed in one list; it just rarely means anything. No `href` argument, and that is the point rather than an omission: an entry that navigates is a row, and a row is Item.
