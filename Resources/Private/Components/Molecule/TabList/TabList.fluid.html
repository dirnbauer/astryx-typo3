TabList — the strip of triggers above a set of panels. role="tablist" is not redundant on a `div`: the element means nothing on its own, and astryx.js reads the buttons inside rather than the row itself, so the role is the only thing that tells assistive technology what this is. The accessible name matters for the same reason — a page with two unnamed tablists offers a reader two identical rows and no way to tell them apart. aria-orientation is emitted alongside `data-orientation` because the arrow keys a screen reader announces have to agree with the axis the stylesheet lays the tabs out on.
