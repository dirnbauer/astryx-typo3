Stat — one figure and what it counts: "98 %", "uptime", "over the last twelve months". Nine elements built this by hand from two or three Text atoms and a few lines of their own CSS each, and every one of them made the same two decisions, which are the reason this is a component: - it is a description list group. The label is the term and the figure is its description, in that order in the source, so the pair survives being read out of context; a list of stats announced as loose text has no count and no pairing. - it reads figure first. The figure is what the eye is meant to land on, so it is moved above its label on screen while the source keeps the term first. `as="div"` (the default) is the group for a parent description list — a Grid or a MetadataList rendered as a `dl`, holding one Stat per figure. `as="dl"` makes the stat a description list of its own, for a stat that stands alone: in a card, beside a quote, in a band of one. The four parts are named slots as well as arguments, and the slot wins, as on SectionIntro: a slot carries the markup f:render.text returns, which is what makes the part editable on the page; a string argument would arrive escaped. `value` may also be the default slot when no part is named. value the figure, at a display size and in tabular digits unit after the figure on its baseline: "%", "ms", "per month" label the term: what the figure counts note a quieter line under the label: the period, the source `size` sets the figure — `sm` display-3, `md` display-2, `lg` display-1 — and the unit follows it. `color="accent"` sets the figure in the accent text colour. `align="center"` centres the stat. `rule="top"` draws a hairline above it, the way a row of figures is ruled off from the copy above it; with two columns a rule between figures would land in the middle of the band, so the rule belongs to each figure instead. Every part is tested against the empty string, never for truth: a figure of "0" is a figure ("0 incidents"), and Fluid reads "0" as false. Without a label there is no term, and a `dd` without its `dt` is invalid: the stat is then a paragraph of spans — `as="dl"` becomes a `p`, the figure and the note are spans. As the group in a parent description list (`as="div"`) a stat needs its label; the parent list is the caller's. The four parts, rendered once and placed by the shapes below. {value} {unit} {label}
