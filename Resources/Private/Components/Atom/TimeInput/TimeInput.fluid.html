TimeInput — a time of day, with no date attached. ``. The field has one segment fewer than a date, so the stylesheet gives it a narrower floor than DateInput's; left at 180px it sat in a box half of which was empty. The value is 24-hour — `18:30` — no matter what the field shows. Whether the visitor sees "6:30 PM" or "18:30" is their locale's business and not something the markup can or should decide, which is the whole argument for using the native control here. `step="1"` adds seconds; anything under 60 does. That is worth knowing before passing a step meant to snap the field to quarter hours — `step` counts seconds, so quarter hours are `900`.
