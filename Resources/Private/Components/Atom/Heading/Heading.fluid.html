Heading — one heading, at a chosen outline level and a chosen visual size. The two are deliberately separate arguments. `level` decides which element is emitted, and therefore what a screen reader announces and where the heading lands in the document outline; `type` decides only how big it looks. A page that needs an h2 to read as display-sized says level="2" type="display-2" rather than lying about its level, which is the single most common way a design system breaks heading structure. data-level is upstream's own attribute name since Astryx v0.6.0.
