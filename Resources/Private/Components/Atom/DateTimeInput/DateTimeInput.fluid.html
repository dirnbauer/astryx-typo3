DateTimeInput — a day and a time in one field. `type="datetime-local"`, which is the only one of the two that browsers still implement: plain `type="datetime"` was removed from HTML years ago and every engine falls back to a text box, which looks identical in the backend and loses the picker on the page. "Local" is the part worth stating. The field posts `2026-09-19T18:30` with no zone at all, so whatever reads it decides which zone that was. For an event on a site with one venue that is right and simple; for anything booked across zones the form needs to say which zone it means, and this component cannot say it — put it in the label. `step` is what decides whether the field offers seconds. Left unset the browser rounds to the minute, which is what a start time wants.
