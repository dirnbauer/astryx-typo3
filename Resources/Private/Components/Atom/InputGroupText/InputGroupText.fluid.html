InputGroupText — the segment of an InputGroup that is not a control: a currency sign, a fixed URL prefix, a unit. InputGroup writes this class itself around whatever fills its `addon` slot, which covers the leading segment. This component is for the other positions, where the caller is composing the group's children in order and the segment happens to fall after the field. `ariaHidden` is here because a prefix is very often the label said twice. "https://" in front of a field already labelled "Website address" is decoration for a screen reader, and hiding it is the difference between a name and a name read out as punctuation. It stays a choice, because a currency sign in front of a bare amount is the only place the currency is stated and must be read.
