NavIcon — the round accent tile a navigation heading wears in front of its title: a product mark, a workspace initial, a section glyph. It is the box and the tone, never the drawing. An Atom composes nothing, so the glyph arrives through the slot as an inline `svg` — which is what `.astryx-nav-icon > svg` sizes — or as a letter or two of text. Hidden from assistive technology unless told otherwise, because everywhere the stylesheet puts one the heading's own title is right beside it, and a mark that repeats the words next to it is simply read out twice. A NavIcon that really is the only label says so with `ariaLabel`: that is what turns it into an image with a name, and it clears the aria-hidden at the same time, since a hidden element with a name is a name nobody hears.
