StatusDot — the small coloured disc that marks a state. Colour is the whole of what this draws, and colour alone is not a message: anyone who cannot tell green from amber is told nothing by it. The dot is therefore hidden from assistive technology by default, on the assumption that the words beside it already say "operational" or "degraded" — which is how every status element in the theme uses it. Where there genuinely is no such label, `ariaLabel` promotes the dot to an image with a name of its own. That is the exception, and stating it explicitly is what keeps it one.
