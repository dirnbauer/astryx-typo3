AvatarStatusDot — the small disc pinned to an avatar's edge. Not Atom/StatusDot with different coordinates. StatusDot is a dot in a line of text, sized in its own right and placed by whatever is around it; this one is positioned against the avatar it sits in — the stylesheet derives its diameter, its inset and its surface-coloured ring from `--_avatar-size`, so it only means anything inside an `.astryx-avatar`. Put it in the Avatar's slot, after the picture. Colour is the whole of what it draws, and colour alone is not a message: the avatar's own label is what says "online", and this repeats it in green. So it is `aria-hidden="true"` by default, and `ariaLabel` is the same escape hatch StatusDot has for the rare case where there is genuinely no label beside it — it promotes the dot to an image with a name of its own. The variants upstream styles are `success` and `error`; `neutral` is this extension's, and it is hollow, so it needs the glyph slot to be legible against the surface. The glyph belongs to the caller because an Atom cannot compose Atom/Icon — pass an inline `svg`, `aria-hidden` like the dot around it.
