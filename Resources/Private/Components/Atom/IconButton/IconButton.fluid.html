IconButton — a square control whose entire label is its glyph. The label is the argument that matters here. A glyph has no accessible name of its own and the icon inside is drawn `aria-hidden`, so without `ariaLabel` this control is announced as "button" and nothing more. It is required in practice even though Fluid cannot make it so. `position` is for the pair that sits at the two ends of the thing they control — the previous and next handles of a carousel — and is what the surrounding component reads to place them and point them the right way.
