IconButton — a square control whose entire label is its glyph. The label is the argument that matters here. A glyph has no accessible name of its own and the icon inside is drawn `aria-hidden`, so without `ariaLabel` this control is announced as "button" and nothing more. It is required in practice even though Fluid cannot make it so. `position` is for the pair that sits at the two ends of the thing they control — the previous and next handles of a carousel — and is what the surrounding component reads to place them and point them the right way. `carousel` and `dialogOpen` are the two script hooks a glyph-only control carries in this theme: astryx.js pages a rail by `data-g-carousel-prev` and `data-g-carousel-next`, and opens a modal by the id in `data-g-dialog-open`. They are declared arguments rather than attributes a caller writes beside the component because Fluid drops what a component does not declare — which is how both carousels ended up with arrows that rendered and did nothing. Both are written only when asked for: an empty `data-g-dialog-open` would match the script's selector and bind a click that opens nothing, and a bare hook on every icon button is worse than no hook at all. Only the `button` forms carry them, because neither an arrow nor a modal opener navigates.
