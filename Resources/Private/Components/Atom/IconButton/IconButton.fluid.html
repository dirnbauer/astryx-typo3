IconButton — a square control whose whole label is its glyph. The label is the argument that matters. A glyph has no accessible name of its own, and the icon inside is drawn `aria-hidden`, so without `ariaLabel` this control is announced as "button" and nothing else. It is required in practice even though Fluid cannot make it so here. `position` is for the pair that sits at the two ends of something it controls — the previous and next handles of a carousel — and is what the surrounding component reads to place and to point them. Like Button, `parameter` or `href` turns the control into an ``, because an icon that navigates is still a link and has to keep middle-click and the screen reader's link list.
