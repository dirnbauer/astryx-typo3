Badge — a small pill that classifies or states. Colour alone is never the message. The variants borrow the theme's categorical quartet — background, border, icon and text tuned together and checked for contrast — rather than a status colour over its own tint, because in several themes the muted tint resolves to the same value as the colour it tints, and the label is then painted onto itself. Whatever the variant, the badge still carries its meaning as words.
