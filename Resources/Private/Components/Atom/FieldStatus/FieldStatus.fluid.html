FieldStatus — what went wrong, or what went right, under a control. A `

` with `aria-live="polite"`. A validation message that appears after the page has settled is new information, and new information that is only painted a colour is not delivered to anyone using a screen reader. Polite rather than assertive: a form usually reports several fields at once, and assertive would interrupt the reading of each with the next. The type is `data-type`, which is upstream's own axis for this component — the compiled themes carry `.astryx-field-status[data-type=…]` rules, so the attribute is what lets a theme's own tints land on this markup. Field, for historical reasons, still writes the status as a second class on the same element; both work, and this is the one the contract describes. Nothing here is coloured only. The tint says "error" to people who see it, the words in the slot say it to everyone, and the caller points the control's `aria-describedby` at this element's id so the two arrive together.
