Skeleton — the grey shape standing in for content that has not arrived. It is always hidden from assistive technology, with no argument to say otherwise. A skeleton has no content to announce: read aloud it is an empty box, and a screenful of them would be a screenful of nothing. The fact that something is loading belongs on the region that is waiting, as `aria-busy`, where it is said once instead of once per placeholder. `shape` matters more than it looks. A text skeleton takes its height from the line height of the body text rather than from a fixed number, so a run of them occupies exactly the space the sentences will, and the page does not jump when the real text replaces them.
