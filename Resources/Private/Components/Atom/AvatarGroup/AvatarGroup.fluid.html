AvatarGroup — a cluster of portraits that overlap, for saying "these people" without saying each of them. The size is set here rather than on each Avatar inside, because the overlap is derived from it: the stylesheet reads `--_avatar-size` off this element and pulls every child but the first back by a share of it. An avatar sizing itself would put the picture and the overlap out of step. The size reaches the markup as `data-size`, like every other modifier in this theme; the stylesheet was written against `.astryx-avatar-group.md` until 2.0 and now reads `[data-size="md"]`. A pile of portraits is usually decoration, with the count written out in words beside it. `ariaHidden` is how the caller says so, and most do.
