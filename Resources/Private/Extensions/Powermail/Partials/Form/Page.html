One Powermail page: a fieldset whose legend names it, and a FormLayout holding its fields. The legend is visible when the form has more than one page — then it is the heading of a step — and visually hidden otherwise, where it would only repeat the form title above it. It is never dropped: the fieldset needs a name either way. `powermail_fieldset` and `powermail_fieldset_{uid}` are the hooks the multi-step script and powermail_cond show and hide pages by. In a multi-step form the last page's submit button moves out of the field list and into the step row, next to "Back", so the row reads the same on every page.
