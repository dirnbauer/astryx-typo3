Powermail "radio" field: an Astryx RadioList — a fieldset whose legend is the field title — with one RadioListItem row per option, with the drawn radio. The row is the label, so the whole line is the hit target. The options are f:form.radio rather than RadioListItem itself: the ViewHelper is what registers the name with Powermail's request token. Powermail's client validation needs two selectors for a group, and both are pointed at Astryx markup here: the message goes into the field's message slot, and the error class lands on the fieldset. The ids stay powermail_field_{marker}_{n}, which Powermail's error summary links to. {f:if(condition: field.description, then: '{fieldId}-description')} {f:if(condition: invalid, then: '{fieldId}-message')} {field.title} {field.title}{f:translate(key: '{l}:field.required')}
