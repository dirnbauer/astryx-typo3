The Powermail form, as an Astryx Card holding one real form. What stays exactly as Powermail wrote it is everything its scripts and powermail_cond read: the `powermail_form`, `powermail_morestep`, `powermail_fieldset*` and `powermail_form_uid` hooks, the data attributes from enableJavascriptValidationAndAjax, and the step buttons' data-powermail-morestep-* attributes. Those are behaviour, not styling — the look is the Astryx components and the `g-form` rules only. Multi-step forms: Powermail's script shows one fieldset at a time and moves an `active` class between the step buttons, so the step list below is plain buttons it can find by attribute. A click has to land on the button itself (the script reads the target's attributes), which is why the number and title inside it take no pointer events.
